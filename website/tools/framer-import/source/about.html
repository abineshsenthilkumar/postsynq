Our story

We know what it's like to build a business while trying to keep up with social media. Between creating content, designing posts and staying consistent, it can become a job of its own. That's why we built PostSynq.

When our founder, Preetham, started building the company, we were a small team wearing many hats, including our own social media.

Creating posts, writing captions and publishing on every platform took hours each week. That challenge became PostSynq.

Building a business is already a full-time job. Social media shouldn't feel like another one.

We experienced how much effort goes into keeping a social media presence alive. One day you’re thinking about content ideas. The next, you’re designing creatives, writing captions, scheduling posts and switching between platforms.

For startups and small teams, every task adds up and takes time away from building the actual business. So we asked ourselves: what if it could all be managed from one place, with AI doing more of the heavy lifting? Here’s what PostSynq brings together:

Content creation? ✓ Check

AI-generated visuals? ✓ Check

A planned content calendar? ✓ Check

Scheduling and publishing? ✓ Check

Conversations with your audience? ✓ Check

Performance insights? ✓ Check

All in one platform, instead of a different tool for every task.

Then we went a step further and built Synq AI, our AI-powered social media assistant. Tell it what you need, give it your brand context, and let it get the work moving. Because managing social media should start with an idea, not a hundred manual steps.

Our vision is to make intelligent social media management accessible to every business, so people can focus on creativity, relationships and growth. PostSynq is built by AtSpeed Innovations, a growing team in Hyderabad. And this is just the beginning.
