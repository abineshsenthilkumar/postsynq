Privacy Policy

  1. Introduction

At PostSynq, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This page explains in plain language what information we collect when you use our AI-powered social media management platform, how we use it, and the choices you have. PostSynq is a product of AtSpeed Innovations, Hyderabad.

  1. Personal information

We collect the information you give us when you sign up, such as your name, email address and business details. When you connect social media accounts, we receive the access needed to publish, read comments and show insights for those accounts. We also store the content you create, upload or schedule in PostSynq.

  1. Usage

We use your information to run PostSynq: to create and publish your posts, power Synq AI with your brand context, show your analytics, manage your account and billing, and send important updates about the service. We do not sell your personal information.

  1. Data Protection

We protect your information with industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted connections and restricted access to account data. Social accounts are connected through each platform's official login, and you can disconnect them at any time from your settings.

  1. User Rights

You stay in control of your information. You can view and update your account details, disconnect social accounts, delete content you have created, or ask us to delete your account. To make a request, email support@postsynq.in.

  1. Data Sharing

PostSynq shares information only when needed to provide the service. This includes the social media platforms you choose to publish to, and trusted service providers that help us run PostSynq, such as hosting, payments and AI processing. We may also share information when required by law.

  1. Tracking

Our website and app use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences and understand how PostSynq is used, so we can improve it. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

  1. Data Handling

We keep your information only as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information within a reasonable period, unless we are required to keep it.

  1. Privacy

We may update this privacy notice as PostSynq grows. When we make important changes, we will let you know through the app or by email. For our complete privacy policy, visit postsynq.in/legal/privacy.

  1. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy? Email us at support@postsynq.in, call +91 95821 22999, or write to us at Cokarma, Kokapet, Hyderabad.
